KVENT

One-page rules. Same flow as the classic card game of the Witcher 3 tavern, but with our own cards, names and art. (Classic names in brackets.)

How a match works

  • Best of 3 rounds. Each player has 2 hearts (lives). A round is won by the higher total; the loser loses a heart.
  • Tie: both lose a heart. Only the Blånisser win ties (two Blånisser: both lose).
  • Round 1: a coin toss decides who starts (Rødnisser may choose). Rounds 2 and 3: the winner of the last round starts (setting START_RULE, see below).
  • You get 10 cards and may redraw 2, once. No new cards in later rounds (only faction powers draw).
  • On your turn: play ONE card or pass. A pass ends the round for you; the other player may keep playing. Out of cards = passed.
  • When a round ends, all cards go to the discard pile (weather and horns too). Night Creatures keep one random unit.
  • Deck: at least 22 unit cards, at most 10 specials, exactly 1 leader (once per match).

Who starts rounds 2 and 3 (START_RULE)

  • winner (default, classic): the winner of the last round starts. After a tie the player who did not start swaps in.
  • loser: the loser of the last round starts.
  • alternate: the players take turns.
  • toss: coin toss every round.
Change it on the Kvent menu if you remember it differently. Now selected: winner.

Order of scoring in a row

  1. Weather: unit power becomes 1
  2. Pals: multiply by number of same-name units
  3. Cheer: +1 per Cheer unit in the row
  4. Horn: double
Legends (gold) skip all of it.

Unit abilities

Turncoat(Spy) goes on the OPPONENT's row and counts for him, you draw 2. At round end it goes to the graveyard of the side it stood on. A Dummy can take it back.
Healer(Medic) revive a non-Legend from your own discard at once; a revived Healer chains
Pals(Tight Bond) n units with the same name: each is multiplied by n
Cheer(Morale Boost) +1 to the other units in the row
Gang(Muster) pulls the whole gang from your deck AND hand
Horn unitdoubles the other units in the row
Blaze unit(Scorch unit) if the enemy row (same row) totals 10+, burn its strongest unit(s)
Legend(Hero) immune to weather, horn, blaze, dummy and Healers

Special cards

Snowstorm / Fog / Rain(Biting Frost / Impenetrable Fog / Torrential Rain) the matching row of BOTH sides: every non-Legend unit counts 1
Thaw(Clear Weather) ends all weather
Yule Horn(Commander's Horn) doubles one row of yours (not Legends); one horn per row, card or leader
Brann!(Scorch) burns the strongest non-Legend unit(s) on the WHOLE board, all that tie, both sides
Lokkedukke(Decoy) swap with a non-Legend unit on YOUR side of the board; it returns to your hand

Factions (one passive each)

Town Folk(Northern Realms) draw a card after you WIN a round
Rødnisser(Scoia'tael) you choose who starts round 1
Blånisser(Nilfgaard) you WIN ties
Night Creatures(Monsters) after every round one random unit of yours stays
Fjord Folk(Skellige) at the start of round 3, 2 random units from your discard pile return to the board
Neutral cards fit every deck.

Leaders (18, once per match, costs your turn)

Each deck has exactly one. Passive leaders have no button.

Quick tips

  • Do not spend everything in round 1. Losing a round on purpose can win the match.
  • Pass when you are comfortably ahead and the other player has more cards left to waste.
  • Save Healers and Horns for when they matter. Play spies early.
  • Check weather before you pile units on a row.

Controls

  • Mouse/touch: click a card, click the glowing row (or the card again). Hover or long-press to read a card.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter select, Esc back/pass, Z zoom, P pass, L leader.
  • PS5 pad: D-pad/stick move, Cross select, Circle back/pass, Square zoom, Triangle leader.
