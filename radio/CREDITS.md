## HEKSA FM (66.6)

- radio/heksa/musicbox.m4a: "Silent Night" (Franz Gruber, 1818, public domain) as a slowing, broken music box, synthesised for this game (numpy).
- radio/heksa/static.m4a: radio static, mains hum and drone, synthesised for this game.
- radio/heksa/hx*.m4a: witch station lines written for this game, Microsoft Edge text-to-speech (en-US-JennyNeural) with the witch voice filter.
- voice/witch_*.mp4: the game's existing witch voice lines (reused).

Kor & Kirke also plays the carolers' recordings in carols/ (see carols/CREDITS.md).
